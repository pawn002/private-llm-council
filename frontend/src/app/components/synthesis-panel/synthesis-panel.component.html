The confidence assessment is produced by a second LLM call — a separate model evaluates the synthesis after it has been written, based on how many council members responded and how strongly they disagreed.

Synthesis Quality

How much you should trust the synthesis. High means the council converged — perspectives agreed and the synthesis reliably represents the group's view. Low means the council was divided enough that the synthesis may be glossing over genuine conflict.

Dissent Strength

How strongly minority positions held their ground. High means there are meaningful alternative views not folded into the synthesis — check the Minority Reports section. Low means dissent was weak or absent.

Reading them together

Synthesis QualityDissent StrengthRead as
HighLowStrong consensus — trust the synthesis
HighHighCoherent synthesis, but meaningful dissent — check Minority Reports
LowHighGenuinely divided council — treat synthesis as one view, not a conclusion
LowLowThin or low-quality response set

Note: this is a model assessing another model's output — treat scores as approximate signals, not precise measurements.
